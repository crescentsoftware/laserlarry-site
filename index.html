For parents

Silly and safe

Here is what you should know before your kids play.

Cartoon action, no gore

Enemies are bugs, rocks and grumpy planets. They pop. Nothing bleeds.

No chat, no strangers

There is no chat and no way to message other players. Leaderboards use Game Center nicknames.

Purchases go through Apple

Coin packs are optional. Turn on Ask to Buy in Family Sharing, or limit purchases in Screen Time, and your child needs your OK to buy.

No personal information

We don't ask for names, emails, photos or location. Read the details in our privacy policy.

Support

Need help?

Found a bug, lost your coins, or have an idea? Email us and a real person will answer, usually within two business days.

Email info@laserlarrygame.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

I bought coins but they didn't show up.

Close the game completely and open it again, with an internet connection. The game finishes any interrupted purchase when it starts. Still missing? Email us with the date of the purchase from your Apple receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone?

Progress and coins are saved on your device, so they don't move to a new phone. Purchases that unlock something for good can be brought back with Restore Purchases in the game's store, on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I stop my child from buying things?

Use Ask to Buy in Family Sharing, or go to Settings, Screen Time, Content & Privacy Restrictions, and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow.

Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Effective [date]. Applies to the Laser Larry app and this website.

Laser Larry is made by Crescent Software in Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada ("we", "us"). Kids play Laser Larry, so we keep the data we collect to the minimum the game needs to work and to fix crashes. We do not sell personal information, and we do not ask players for their name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

The random install ID is created when the game is first opened. It is not linked to your name, your Apple Account, or your advertising ID, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Crash reports are set up to strip IP addresses and other personal data before they are sent.

Children

Laser Larry is made for a general audience that includes children under 13. For every player, we do not collect personal information as defined by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or Canada's PIPEDA. We tell our ad and analytics partners to treat the app as child-directed where a player may be a child, which means ads are not personalized and are not based on what the player does in other apps.

If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

Ads and tracking

The game may show ads from Unity Ads, including optional videos you can watch for a reward. We do not track players across other companies' apps or websites. If the game ever asks for permission to track on iOS, you can say no and the game works the same. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the game. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Apple, for the App Store, in-app purchases and Game Center: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Unity, for ads and analytics: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Sentry, for crash reports: sentry.io/privacy

These providers may process data outside Canada, including in the United States.

How long we keep data

Crash reports and analytics events are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Support emails are kept until your question is resolved and then for up to one year.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to delete your game progress and the install ID from your device.
  • Email us to ask what data we hold, or to have it deleted. Because we don't know who you are, we may need details such as your device model and roughly when you played to find it.
  • Turn off in-app purchases or tracking in iOS Screen Time and Privacy settings.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from this site rather than a third party. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects what we collect from players, we will say so in the app's release notes.
